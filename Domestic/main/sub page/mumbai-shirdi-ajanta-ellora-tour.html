Overview

Embark on a journey that transcends time. This 5-day package takes you from the bustling metropolis of Mumbai to the serene abode of Sai Baba in Shirdi, and finally to the ancient rock-cut wonders of Ajanta and Ellora Caves. Witness the architectural brilliance of the past and find spiritual solace in one of India's most revered pilgrimage sites.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Mumbai

Pick up from Mumbai Airport/Railway Station. Transfer to hotel. Evening visit to Gateway of India and Marine Drive. Overnight stay in Mumbai.

Day 02
Mumbai Local & Drive to Shirdi

Morning visit to Siddhivinayak Temple and Haji Ali Dargah. Later, drive to Shirdi (approx. 5-6 hours). Upon arrival, check-in to hotel. Overnight in Shirdi.

Day 03
Shirdi Darshan & Transfer to Aurangabad

Early morning visit to Sai Baba Temple for Darshan and Aarti. Visit Dwarkamai and Chavadi. Post lunch, drive to Aurangabad (approx. 3 hours). En route visit Shani Shingnapur. Overnight in Aurangabad.

Day 04
Ajanta Caves Excursion

Full day excursion to Ajanta Caves (closed on Mondays). These 2nd-century rock-cut caves are famous for their magnificent paintings and sculptures depicting the life of Buddha. Return to Aurangabad for overnight stay.

Day 05
Ellora Caves & Departure

Morning visit to Ellora Caves (closed on Tuesdays), home to the majestic Kailasa Temple. Also visit Daulatabad Fort en route. Transfer to Mumbai Airport/Railway Station for return journey.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Dress Code:

Wear modest clothing for temples. Carry a shawl or dupatta for women. For caves, wear sturdy walking shoes.

Photography:

Photography is allowed in caves (with a small fee) but strictly prohibited inside the sanctum of Shirdi Temple.

City Traffic:

Mumbai traffic is unpredictable. We schedule local sightseeing with buffer time to avoid missing flights/trains.

Walking:

Be prepared for moderate walking at the cave sites. Ajanta requires a short bus ride from the parking area.

Important Travel Guidelines
  • Cave Timings: Ajanta Caves are closed on Mondays, and Ellora Caves are closed on Tuesdays. Please plan your travel dates accordingly.
  • Shirdi Rules: Mobile phones and cameras are strictly not allowed inside the Samadhi Mandir in Shirdi. Deposit facilities are available.
  • Shani Shingnapur: Men are required to remove their shirts before entering the temple premises.
  • Vehicle Type: For groups of 4-6, an Innova is recommended for comfort on the Mumbai-Shirdi highway.
