Overview

Embark on a divine journey to the holy abode of Sai Baba. Shirdi is one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in India, where devotees experience peace and miracles. Our package ensures a hassle-free darshan experience, comfortable stay, and a visit to the powerful Shani Shingnapur temple.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival & Temple Visit

Pick up from Mumbai/Pune Airport or Railway Station. Drive to Shirdi (4-5 hrs). Before entering Shirdi, you may stop for lunch. Check-in to hotel. Evening visit to Samadhi Mandir for Darshan and attend the Dhoop Aarti (Sunset) or Shej Aarti (Night). Overnight stay.

Day 02
Shani Shingnapur Excursion

Early morning Kakad Aarti (Optional). Visit Dwarkamai, Chavadi, Gurusthan, and Lendi Baug. After breakfast, proceed to Shani Shingnapur (70 km). Darshan of Lord Shani Dev. Return to Shirdi by evening. Free time for shopping in the temple market. Overnight stay.

Day 03
Departure

Morning breakfast. Check-out from hotel. Visit Sai Heritage Village (Optional). Proceed back to Mumbai/Pune for your return flight/train with blessed memories.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: Driving to Shirdi can be tiring for the driver. Our chauffeur-driven package allows your entire family to travel safely and focus on prayers. We also ensure you visit Shani Shingnapur, which is often missed due to lack of local transport knowledge.

Pilgrim's Guide & Tips

Mobiles:

Mobile phones and cameras are strictly prohibited inside the Samadhi Mandir. Lockers are available.

Footwear:

You have to remove footwear at the designated stands before entering the temple complex.

Important
  • Darshan Booking: We highly recommend booking Darshan slots online on the official Sansthan website to avoid long queues.
  • Thursday Rush: Thursdays are very crowded. Plan your visit on weekdays for a quicker darshan.
