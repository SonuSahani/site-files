Overview

Indulge in a premium holiday experience in the Eastern Himalayas. This exclusive package combines the colonial elegance of Darjeeling's tea estates with the modern luxury of Gangtok's mountain resorts. Wake up to the view of Mt. Kanchenjunga, sip the world's finest tea, and rejuvenate in upscale comfort.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival & Transfer to Gangtok

Pick up from NJP/Bagdogra. Scenic luxury drive to Gangtok (approx. 4.5 hrs). Check-in to your premium mountain-view resort. Evening at leisure to enjoy resort amenities or stroll on MG Marg. Overnight stay in Gangtok.

Day 02
Gangtok Sightseeing (Tsomgo Lake)

Excursion to Tsomgo Lake and Baba Mandir aboard a comfortable vehicle. Enjoy the snow (seasonal) and yak rides. Return to resort for a warm dinner. Overnight in Gangtok.

Day 03
Gangtok to Darjeeling via Tea Gardens

Morning drive to Darjeeling (approx. 4 hrs). En route visit Peshok Tea Garden and Lovers Meet View Point. Check-in to a heritage resort in Darjeeling. Evening free for Mall Road. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 04
Tiger Hill Sunrise & Local Tour

Early morning sunrise tour to Tiger Hill to view Kanchenjunga. Visit Batasia Loop and Ghoom Monastery. Post breakfast visit Zoo, HMI, and Japanese Temple. Overnight at Darjeeling resort.

Day 05
Departure

Breakfast at the resort. Transfer to NJP/Bagdogra for your return journey with fond memories of luxury and landscapes.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Smart casuals for dinners. Warm layers are essential, even in summer evenings in Darjeeling.

Roads:

Mountain roads can be bumpy. Our luxury vehicles ensure better suspension and comfort.

Documents:

Carry 2 PP photos and Original ID (Voter ID/Passport) for Sikkim entry permits.

Cuisine:

Try local Thukpa and Momos, but our resorts also serve distinctive Continental and Indian fare.

Important Guidelines
  • Permit Regulations: Aadhar Card is NOT valid for Sikkim permits. Please carry Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport.
  • Nathula Pass: Nathula is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. Permit issuance is subject to army regulations.
