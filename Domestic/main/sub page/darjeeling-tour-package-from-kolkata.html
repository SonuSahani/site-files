Overview

Witness the majestic Kanchenjunga from the Queen of Hills. This tour takes you through the verdant tea gardens, the heritage Toy Train (UNESCO site), and the diverse culture of Gorkhaland. Experience the golden sunrise at Tiger Hill and the serenity of the Peace Pagoda.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Darjeeling

Pick up from NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport. Scenic drive through the tea gardens to Darjeeling. Check-in and evening leisure at the Mall Road.

Day 02
Tiger Hill Sunrise

Early morning drive to Tiger Hill (4 AM) for a breathtaking sunrise over Mt. Kanchenjunga. On return visit Batasia Loop and Ghoom Monastery.

Day 03
Darjeeling Sightseeing

Visit P.N. Zoological Park, Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI), Ropeway, and Happy Valley Tea Garden. Enjoy a ride on the DHR Toy Train (Joy Ride - Optional).

Day 04
Departure

Morning breakfast. Transfer back to NJP/Bagdogra with sweet memories of the Queen of Hills.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: This is the classic Eastern Himalayan experience. We recommend it because it perfectly balances heritage (Toy Train), nature (Tea Gardens), and magnificent views (Tiger Hill). It's a relaxed itinerary suitable for all ages.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Carry light woolens for summer and heavy woolens, gloves, and caps for winter. Comfortable walking shoes are essential.

Travel Time:

The drive from NJP/Bagdogra to Darjeeling takes 3-4 hours. Roads can be curvy, so carry motion sickness meds if needed.

Documents:

Carry original Voter ID/Aadhar Card and passport size photos for hotel check-ins and permits if visiting border areas.

Toy Train:

Joy ride tickets sell out fast. Book engaging in advance or ask us to include it in your package.

Important Guidelines
  • Tiger Hill: Departure for Tiger Hill is usually at 4:00 AM. It gets very cold, so dress warmly.
  • Traffic: Darjeeling town can have traffic congestion. Please coordinate with your driver for pickup timings.
  • Water: Water scarcity can occur in hills; please use water judiciously at hotels.
  • Cleanliness: Darjeeling is a plastic-free zone. Please do not litter.
