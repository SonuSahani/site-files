Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival & Cellular Jail

Pick up from Port Blair Airport. Check-in to hotel. After lunch, visit the National Memorial Cellular Jail. In the evening, witness the moving Light & Sound Show.

Day 02
Havelock & Radhanagar Beach

Ferry to Havelock Island. Afternoon visit to Radhanagar Beach, ideal for swimming and sand castles. Safe and shallow waters for kids.

Day 03
Elephant Beach - Water Sports

Boat ride to Elephant Beach. Enjoy a complimentary Glass Bottom Boat ride to see corals without getting wet. Optional water sports available.

Day 04
Kalapathar Beach & Return to Port Blair

Morning visit to the scenic Kalapathar Beach. Later, take the ferry back to Port Blair. Evening free for local shopping.

Day 05
Departure

Transfer to Airport with happy family memories.

A Tale of the Tides

"The locals whisper that the sea around Havelock remembers everything. Legend says that the first sailors to these shores didn't anchor their boats; the calm turquoise waters simply held them in a warm embrace. On quiet nights at Radhanagar Beach, if you listen closely past the gentle lapping of waves, you can hear the ocean singing the same lullaby that put those ancient mariners to sleep under the starlit canopy. This isn't just a holiday; it's a chance to let the sea hum its song to you."

Our View

Why we recommend this for families: We've designed this itinerary with 'Pace & Patience' in mind. No 5 AM wake-up calls (unless you want the sunrise!). The transfers are private, so you aren't waiting for a group. The beaches selected like Radhanagar and Bharatpur have shallow waters, making them safe for kids and seniors alike.

Parent's Guide & Tips

Food for Kids:

Most restaurants serve plain rice, dal, and noodles. Carry Cerelac/formula if needed; shops may close early.

Seasickness:

Ferries can be rocky. Give kids Avomine (consult doctor) 30 mins before boarding.

Beach Safety:

Always swim in designated zones with lifeguards. Radhanagar is the safest.

Timing:

Start early (8 AM) to avoid the midday sun, especially with children.

Important Guidelines for Families
  • Check-in/Out: Hotel check-in is usually 12:00 PM and check-out is 09:00 AM.
  • Weather & Ferries: Schedules are subject to weather. A buffer day is recommended.
  • Eco-Friendly: Plastic bags are banned in Andamans. Please carry reusable bottles/bags.
  • For Kids: Carry hats, rash guards, and kid-safe sunscreen. The tropical sun is strong.
  • Health: Keep emergency medicines handy (Crocin, Band-aids, ORS, Avomine for motion sickness).
