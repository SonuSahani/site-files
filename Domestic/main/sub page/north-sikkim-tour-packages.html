Overview

Escape to the Land of Snow-capped peaks and pristine lakes. This 5-day North Sikkim package takes you to the breathtaking Gurudongmar Lake (one of the highest in the world), the flower valley of Yumthang, and the snowy plains of Zero Point. Perfectly curated for nature lovers seeking tranquility and adventure.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Gangtok

Pick up from Bagdogra Airport/NJP Station. Scenic drive to Gangtok (The Capital). Check-in to hotel. Evening leisure at MG Marg (Mahatma Gandhi Marg).

Day 02
Gangtok to Lachen

Drive to Lachen via Seven Sisters Waterfall and Singhik Viewpoint. En route visit Singhik Rangit Vew. Overnight stay in Lachen.

Day 03
Gurudongmar Lake & Lachung

Early morning trip to Gurudongmar Lake (17,800 ft). Visit Thangu Valley & Chopta Valley. Return to Lachen, then drive to Lachung for overnight stay.

Day 04
Yumthang Valley & Zero Point

Morning visit to Yumthang Valley (Valley of Flowers). Proceed to Zero Point (Yumesamdong) to play in the snow. Return to Gangtok in the evening.

Day 05
Departure

Morning breakfast and transfer to Bagdogra Airport/NJP Station for return journey.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: North Sikkim is rugged but rewarding. We handle the tricky permit processes and ensure you have reliable vehicles for the steep gradients. The itinerary is paced to help you acclimatize to the altitude, especially crucial for the Gurudongmar Lake visit.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Carry heavy woolens (thermals, jackets, gloves) even in summer. It is freezing at Zero Point and Gurudongmar.

Altitude:

Drink plenty of water and avoid heavy meals the night before visiting Gurudongmar Lake to prevent AMS.

ID Proofs:

Carry original Voter ID/Passport and 4-6 passport size photos. Permits will not be processed without these.

Eco-Friendly:

North Sikkim is a protected zone. Do not litter or plastic items. Respect local culture.

Important Himalayan Guidelines
  • Permits: Permit issuance is subject to government regulations. We do not guarantee permits if authorities restrict entry.
  • Road Conditions: Landslides are common in monsoon. Buffer days are recommended.
  • Vehicle: SUVs (Innova/Scorpio) are mandatory for North Sikkim routes.
  • Health: People with severe asthma or heart conditions should consult a doctor before traveling to high altitudes.
  • Check-in: Hotels in Lachen/Lachung are basic compared to Gangtok due to infrastructure limitations.
