Overview

Welcome to the land of the Giants. Kaziranga National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, hosting two-thirds of the world's Great One-horned Rhinoceroses. Enjoy thrilling Jeep safaris through the tall elephant grass, spot wild water buffaloes, swamp deer, and if you are lucky, the elusive Royal Bengal Tiger.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival Guwahati to Kaziranga

Meet and greet at Guwahati Airport/Railway Station. Transfer to Kaziranga National Park (5 hrs). Check-in to your jungle resort. Evening free.

Day 02
Jungle Safari Day

Early morning Elephant Safari (5:30 AM/6:30 AM) in the Western/Central zone. Return for breakfast. Afternoon take a Jeep Safari in a different range. Evening visit the Orchid Park to see cultural dance.

Day 03
Kaziranga to Guwahati

After breakfast, drive back to Guwahati. En-route visit Maha Mrityunjay Temple (Nagaon). Check-in at Guwahati hotel. Evening cruise on Brahmaputra river.

Day 04
Departure

Morning visit Kamakhya Temple. Transfer to Guwahati Airport for your onward journey.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: Kaziranga is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a pride of Assam. This package ensures you get the best safari zones (Central/Western) and comfortable stays, maximizing your wildlife spotting chances.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Earth-toned clothes (brown, green) are best for safari. Avoid bright colors.

Photography:

Carry a good zoom lens. Flash photography is strictly prohibited inside the park.

Important Guidelines
  • Safari: Elephant safari slots are limited and need early booking.
  • Silence: Maintain silence during the safari to not disturb animals.
  • Plastic: Do not carry plastic bags inside the national park.

Policies & Terms

  • Cancel >30 days: 80% refund.
  • Cancel 15-30 days: 50% refund.
  • Cancel 0-15 days: No refund.
  • Safari timings and zones are allotted by the Forest Department.
  • Park may close suddenly due to animal census or VIP visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

The park remains open from November to April. It is closed during the monsoon season (May to October) due to floods.

Elephant Safari tickets are limited and issued by the Forest Department. We need to book them in advance, but they are subject to availability.

Kaziranga is about 200 km from Guwahati, which takes approximately 4-5 hours by road.
