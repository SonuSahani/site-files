Overview

Explore the mystical forests of Gorumara, Jaldapara, and Buxa. The Dooars region is famous for its One-Horned Rhino, elephant safaris, and tea gardens. A paradise for wildlife photographers and nature lovers seeking tranquility.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival Lataguri/Gorumara

Pick up from New Mal Jn/NJP. Transfer to Lataguri. Check-in to resort. Evening Jungle Safari at Gorumara National Park (Watchtower visit).

Day 02
Jhalong & Bindu Excursion

Excursion to Jhalong, Bindu, and Samsing. Enjoy the scenic beauty of the rocky river Murti and the Bhutan border views. Overnight in Lataguri.

Day 03
Jaldapara Safari

Drive to Jaldapara. Afternoon jeep safari/elephant safari (subject to availability) to spot One-Horned Rhinos and Bison. Overnight in Jaldapara/Madarihat.

Day 04
Departure

Morning breakfast. Transfer to Hasimara/New Mal Jn/NJP station for return journey.

But as you drive up to Rohtang Pass, the landscape changes dramatically—from colonial elegance to raw, untamed Himalayan wilderness. It is the perfect blend of the past and nature's power."

Our View

Why we recommend this package: This is the classic Himalayan circuit. We recommend it because it offers variety—the laid-back vibe of Shimla and the high-energy adventure of Manali. We ensure the itinerary allows for acclimatization and ample leisure time, unlike rushed tour packages.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Carry light woolens for Shimla and heavy jackets for Manali/Rohtang. Comfortable walking shoes are a must for exploring Mall Road.

Travel Time:

The journey from Shimla to Manali takes about 8 hours. Carry motion sickness medication if you are prone to it on winding roads.

ID Proofs:

Carry original ID proofs and 2-3 passport size photos. Required for hotel check-ins and Rohtang Pass permits.

Rohtang Pass:

Rohtang Pass is closed on Tuesdays. Plan your itinerary accordingly. In peak season, permits sell out fast.

Important Himalayan Guidelines
  • Weather Dependent: Rohtang Pass visit is subject to weather conditions. If the pass is blocked due to snow, we will take you to the nearest snow point.
  • Traffic Regulations: Entry of private/diesel vehicles to Manali city center is restricted at times. We may arrange cabs for local sightseeing if needed.
  • Heaters: Most hotels provide room heaters on a chargeable basis unless specified as included.
  • Ethical Tourism: Please do not litter the pristine valleys. Use dustbins provided.
