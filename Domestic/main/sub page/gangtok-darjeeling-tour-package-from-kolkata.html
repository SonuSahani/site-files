Overview

Embark on a mesmerizing journey to the jewels of the North East. This 6-day tour connects the vibrant Buddhist culture of Gangtok with the colonial charm of Darjeeling. Witness the majesty of Kanchenjunga, the serenity of Tsomgo Lake, and the rolling tea gardens that make this region a paradise for nature lovers.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival & Transfer to Gangtok

Pick up from NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport. Scenic drive to Gangtok (4-5 hrs) winding along the Teesta River. Check-in to hotel. Evening free to roam MG Marg, the cleanest pedestrian street in India. Overnight in Gangtok.

Day 02
Tsomgo Lake & Baba Mandir

Excursion to the glacial Tsomgo Lake (12,400 ft) and Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir. Enjoy a Yak ride at the lake. (Nathula Pass is optional, subject to permit availability and extra cost). Overnight in Gangtok.

Day 03
Gangtok Sightseeing & Transfer to Darjeeling

Half-day sightseeing: Visit Rumtek Monastery, Do Drul Chorten, and the Flower Show. Afternoon transfer to Darjeeling (4 hrs). Drive through the famous tea gardens of Peshok. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 04
Tiger Hill Sunrise & Local Sightseeing

Early morning (4 AM) drive to Tiger Hill to watch the sunrise over Mt. Kanchenjunga. On return, visit Batasia Loop and Ghoom Monastery. After breakfast, visit HMI, Zoo, Tenzing Rock, and the Japanese Temple. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 05
Mirik Lake Excursion

Day trip to Mirik Lake. Enjoy boating and horse riding. Drive through Nepal Border (Pashupati Market) for some shopping. Return to Darjeeling for the evening. Overnight in Darjeeling.

Day 06
Departure

Morning breakfast. Transfer to NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport for your return journey with wonderful memories.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: This is the classic North East combo for a reason. You get the best of both worlds: the organized beauty of Sikkim and the colonial heritage of West Bengal. It's perfectly paced for families and couples who want supreme views without trekking hard.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Permits:

Carry 4-6 passport photos and Voter ID/Passport/Aadhar (for children). Needed for Tsomgo Lake.

Clothing:

Weather is unpredictable. Carry layers, a good windcheater, and comfortable walking shoes.

Food:

Don't miss the Momos, Thukpa, and locally brewed tea. Most hotels serve Indian/Bengali cuisine.

Important Guidelines
  • Plastic Ban: Sikkim has a strict ban on plastic water bottles. Carry reusable bottles.
  • Smoking: Smoking in public places in Gangtok is a punishable offense.
  • Timings: Sunset is early (around 5 PM). Plan your days to start early.
