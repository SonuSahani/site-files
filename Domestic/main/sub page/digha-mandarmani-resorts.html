Overview

Escape the humidity of the city and feel the cool sea breeze. Our Digha and Mandarmani resort packages offer the perfect mix of leisure and fun. Whether you want to take a dip in the pool overlooking the Bay of Bengal, enjoy fresh crab curry, or just walk barefoot on the sand, we have the perfect property for you.

Itinerary (Sample)

Day 01
Arrival & Beach Fun

Arrive at Digha/Mandarmani by Car/Train/Bus. Check-in to your resort. Enjoy a welcome drink. Afternoon dip in the pool or head to the beach for water sports. Evening enjoy the sea breeze with snacks at the resort lawn. Overnight stay.

Day 02
Sightseeing & Relaxation

After breakfast, visit Amrabati Park, Digha Science Centre, and the Mohana (estuary). Enjoy fresh fish lunch. Evening free for shopping at the beach market (Jhinuk Market). Dinner and overnight stay.

Day 03
Check-out

Morning walk on the beach to see the sunrise. Breakfast at resort. Check-out and proceed for your return journey to Kolkata.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Light cotton clothes, sunglasses, hats, and flip-flops. Don't forget swimwear if you plan to use the pool.

Distance:

Digha is about 4.5 hours drive from Kolkata (180 km). Roads are excellent.

Check-in:

Carry original ID proofs for all adults. Couples must provide valid ID.

Alcohol:

Some resorts serve alcohol, others allow you to bring your own. Please check before booking.

Important Beach Guidelines
  • Safe Bathing: Do not venture deep into the sea during high tide. Follow the instructions of the beach guards/police.
  • Cleanliness: Please do not throw plastic or bottles on the beach. Keep the beaches clean.
