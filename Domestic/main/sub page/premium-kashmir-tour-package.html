Overview

Immerse yourself in the ethereal beauty of the "Paradise on Earth." This premium 6-day package offers the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. Stay in a traditional Luxury Houseboat on Dal Lake, ride the world-famous Gulmarg Gondola, stroll through the Mughal Gardens, and witness the serene valleys of Pahalgam and Sonmarg.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Srinagar

Meet at Srinagar Airport. Transfer to the Luxury Houseboat on Dal Lake. Enjoy a relaxing Shikara ride in the evening. Overnight stay in the Houseboat.

Day 02
Srinagar Local to Gulmarg

Morning visit Mughal Gardens (Shalimar, Nishat, Chashme Shahi). After lunch, drive to Gulmarg. Evening enjoy the sunset views. Overnight stay in Gulmarg Hotel.

Day 03
Gulmarg Adventure

Enjoy the Gondola cable car ride (Phase 1 & 2 optional) to Apharwat Peak. Enjoy skiing or snowboarding (seasonal). Return to hotel for overnight stay.

Day 04
Gulmarg to Pahalgam

Drive to Pahalgam (Valley of Shepherds). En route visit Saffron fields and Avantipura ruins. Visit Betaab Valley and Chandanwari. Overnight stay in Pahalgam Hotel.

Day 05
Pahalgam to Sonmarg

Drive to Sonmarg (Meadow of Gold). Visit Thajiwas Glacier, known for its scenic beauty and snow. Enjoy pony rides or a walk to the glacier. Return to Srinagar in the evening.

Day 06
Departure

Morning breakfast and transfer to Srinagar Airport for your return flight with sweet memories.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

ID Proofs:

Carry original ID proof and 4-6 passport-size photos. These are mandatory for houseboat bookings and security checkpoints.

Temperature:

Evenings are chilly even in summer. Carry a light jacket or windcheater. Heavy woolens needed from Oct to March.

Sim Cards:

Prepaid sim cards from other states usually don't work in Kashmir. Buy a local postpaid SIM or rely on your hotel Wi-Fi.

Cash:

While hotels accept cards, carry sufficient cash for pony rides, tips, and local markets in remote areas.

Important Travel Guidelines
  • Security Checkposts: There are frequent security checks on highways. Keep your ID handy and cooperate with officials.
  • Gulmarg Gondola: Tickets are subject to weather and availability. We recommend booking early morning slots.
  • Pony Rides: Pony walas can be persuasive. Hire only if you wish to and negotiate the price clearly beforehand.
  • Photography: Avoid photography near military installations, bridges, and airports.
