Overview

Ring in the New Year amidst the snowy peaks of Himachal! This comprehensive tour covers the colonial charm of Shimla and the adventurous valleys of Manali. Enjoy a special Gala Dinner on New Year's Eve/Christmas, complete with a DJ party, bonfire, and unlimited fun.

Itinerary

Day 01
Delhi to Shimla

Pick up from Delhi Airport/Station. Drive to Shimla (350 km/8 hrs). En route visit Pinjore Garden. Check-in at Shimla hotel. Dinner and rest.

Day 02
Shimla & Kufri Sightseeing

Visit Kufri for snow activities. Return to Shimla. Visit Jakhu Temple, Christ Church, and The Ridge. Evening free for Mall Road strolling.

Day 03
Shimla to Manali

Morning drive to Manali (260 km/8 hrs). Scenic drive via Pandoh Dam and Kullu. Visit Kullu Shawl Factory. Check-in at Manali hotel.

Day 04
Solang Valley & Gala Dinner

Full day at Solang Valley for skiing/paragliding. Return to hotel. Special Celebration Evening: Music, Bonfire, Snacks, and Gala Dinner.

Day 05
Manali Sightseeing

Visit Hadimba Devi Temple, Vashisht Hot Springs, and Van Vihar. Free time for shopping at Manali Mall Road. Leisure evening.

Day 06
Manali to Delhi

Check-out early. Drive back to Delhi (530 km/12 hrs). Drop at Delhi Airport/Railway Station late evening.
