Overview

Discover the rich history and culture of India's Golden Triangle. Explore the capital Delhi's blend of ancient and modern, stand in awe of the Taj Mahal in Agra, and experience the Rajput hospitality in the Pink City, Jaipur. This is the consummate introduction to North India.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Delhi

Pick up from Delhi Airport/Station. Check-in to hotel. Visit India Gate, President House (drive pass), and Qutub Minar. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 02
Delhi Sightseeing to Agra

Morning visit Red Fort, Jama Masjid, and Raj Ghat. Afternoon drive to Agra via Yamuna Expressway (3-4 hrs). Overnight in Agra.

Day 03
Agra to Jaipur

Sunrise visit to Taj Mahal (One of Seven Wonders). Visit Agra Fort. Drive to Jaipur. En-route visit Fatehpur Sikri. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 04
Jaipur Sightseeing

Morning excursion to Amber Fort (Elephant/Jeep ride optional). Photo stop at Jal Mahal. Visit City Palace, Jantar Mantar, and Hawa Mahal. Shopping in local markets.

Day 05
Jaipur to Delhi

Morning leisurely breakfast. Drive back to Delhi (5-6 hrs). Check-in at Delhi hotel. Evening free for shopping at Connaught Place.

Day 06
Departure

Transfer to Delhi Airport/Railway Station for your onward journey.
