Overview

Discover the soul of Eastern India. This 5-day Odisha Golden Triangle package blends spirituality, history, and nature. From the ancient temples of Bhubaneswar and the architectural marvel of the Konark Sun Temple to the serene beaches of Puri and the dolphin-rich waters of Chilika Lake, this journey offers a perfect mix of culture and relaxation.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Bhubaneswar

Pick up from Bhubaneswar Airport/Railway Station. Transfer to hotel. Visit Lingaraj Temple (outside view) and Udayagiri/Khandagiri Caves. Overnight stay in Bhubaneswar.

Day 02
Bhubaneswar to Puri via Konark

Drive to Puri. En route visit the Konark Sun Temple (UNESCO World Heritage Site) and the Chandrabhaga Beach. Arrive in Puri and check-in to hotel. Evening leisure at Puri Golden Beach.

Day 03
Chilika Lake Excursion

Morning drive to Satapada (Chilika Lake). Enjoy a boat ride to spot Irrawaddy Dolphins and visit the Sea Mouth area. Return to Puri. Evening visit to Jagannath Temple (Outside view/Rath). Overnight in Puri.

Day 04
Puri Local & Alarnath

Morning visit to Jagannath Temple for prayers. Later visit local markets and handicrafts. Optional visit to Alarnath Temple. Return to hotel for overnight stay.

Day 05
Departure

Morning breakfast and transfer to Bhubaneswar Airport/Railway Station for return journey.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: Odisha is often overlooked, but it offers the most authentic spiritual experience in India. We have curated this route to minimize travel time while maximizing sightseeing. The mix of Kalinga architecture and the relaxing marine drive makes for a balanced holiday.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Attire:

Wear comfortable cotton clothes. Carry a scarf or dupatta to cover your head when entering temples.

Photography:

Photography is allowed at Konark and beaches, but strictly prohibited inside the sanctum sanctorum of Jagannath Temple.

Weather:

Summers can be humid. Carry sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat for the Konark visit.

Food:

Try the 'Mahaprasad' at Puri and local seafood delicacies. Vegetarian options are widely available.

Important Temple Guidelines
  • Non-Hindus: Entry is restricted inside the main temple of Jagannath. They can view from the rooftop (Roshaghara).
  • Prohibited Items: Leather items (wallets, belts) and mobile phones are strictly not allowed inside the temple premises.
  • Timings: Temples usually close in the afternoon (around 1 PM to 4 PM). Plan your visits accordingly.
  • Beach Safety: Swim only in designated zones on Puri Beach as currents can be strong.
