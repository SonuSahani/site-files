Our View

Summer in India can be harsh, but the hills offer a sanctuary. We've handpicked these destinations not just for their weather, but for their ability to handle tourism comfortably during peak season. Early booking is key!

Traveler's Tips & Trick

  • Packing: Even in May/June, carry light woolens for the evenings.
  • Traffic: Start your days early (7 AM) to avoid traffic jams at tourist spots.
  • Health: Stay hydrated and carry motion sickness meds for winding roads.

Important Guidelines

  • Littering: Strictly prohibited. Help keep the mountains clean.
  • Permits: Rohtang Pass restricts vehicle numbers. Book well in advance.
  • Respect: Photography inside some temples may be restricted.
