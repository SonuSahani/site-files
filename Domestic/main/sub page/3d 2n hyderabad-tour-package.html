Overview

Hyderabad is a fascinating blend of history and modernity. From the historic Charminar and Golconda Fort to the hi-tech Cyberabad and the massive Ramoji Film City, there is something for everyone. And don't forget the world-famous Hyderabadi Biryani!

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival & City Sightseeing

Pick up from Hyderabad Airport/Station. Check-in. Visit Salar Jung Museum, Charminar, and Mecca Masjid. Evening visit Lumbini Park and Laser Show at Hussain Sagar Lake.

Day 02
Ramoji Film City

Full day excursion to Ramoji Film City (30km). Enjoy various film sets, live shows, and adventure rides. Return to hotel in the evening.

Day 03
Golconda Fort & Departure

Visit Golconda Fort and Qutub Shahi Tombs. Shop for pearls at Laad Bazaar. Drop at Airport/Station for departure.
