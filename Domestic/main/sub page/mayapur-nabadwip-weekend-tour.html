Overview

Escape the city for a soul-refreshing weekend in the holy dhams of Mayapur and Nabadwip – the birthplace of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu. Visit the magnificent ISKCON Chandrodaya Temple, Srila Prabhupada Samadhi, serene Ganges ghats and experience the blissful kirtan atmosphere that makes this place truly divine.

Itinerary

Day 01
Kolkata to Mayapur Transfer

Early morning pickup from Kolkata/Howrah. Drive to Mayapur (approx 4 hrs). Check-in at hotel/guesthouse. Afternoon visit ISKCON campus, Srila Prabhupada Samadhi & evening aarti. Overnight in Mayapur.

Day 02
Mayapur & Nabadwip Sightseeing

Early morning Mangala Aarti (optional). Full day tour: Chandrodaya Temple, Yoga Peeth (birthplace of Chaitanya), ferry to Nabadwip – visit Sonar Gouranga Temple, Dhameswar Mahaprabhu Temple & other heritage sites. Evening Ganges aarti. Overnight in Mayapur.

Day 03
Departure

Morning free for personal darshan or campus exploration. After breakfast, drive back to Kolkata with divine memories.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: Just 4 hours from Kolkata, Mayapur-Nabadwip offers the purest spiritual experience in Bengal. We have designed this weekend tour to cover all major sites comfortably with experienced guides who understand Gaudiya tradition.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Attire:

Traditional modest clothing preferred. Carry comfortable footwear for walking.

Photography:

Allowed in campus but restricted near deities. Respect devotee privacy.

Weather:

Winter is best. Carry umbrella during monsoon visits.

Food:

Pure vegetarian prasadam available everywhere. Highly recommended.

Important Campus Guidelines
  • Vegetarian Zone: Strictly no non-veg, onion, garlic, intoxicants or tobacco on campus.
  • Respect: Maintain silence near temple areas and follow devotee etiquette.
  • Timings: Mangala aarti starts very early (around 4:30 AM). Plan accordingly.
  • Safety: Use authorized rickshaws/vehicles inside campus.
