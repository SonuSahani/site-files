Expert's Guide & Tips

Ferry Logistics:

You will take 3 cruises (PB->Hav, Hav->Neil, Neil->PB). Keep your tickets handy and reach jetty 45 mins prior.

Sunrise Alert:

Sunrises in Andamans are early (around 5:00 AM). It's worth waking up for, especially at Sitapur Beach (Neil).

Photography:

Carry a waterproof pouch for your phone for Elephant Beach water sports.

Permits:

Our driver keeps your permits. Keep your ID proofs accessible at all times.

Pro Tip:
  • Pre-book water sports (Scuba/Sea Walk) to avoid long queues at Elephant Beach.
  • Network is weak on Neil Island. Download movies/books for the night.
  • Carry cash for small shops/auto rides in Neil.
