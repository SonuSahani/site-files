Overview

Welcome to Goa, the land of sun, sand, and spices! This tour is designed to give you the perfect mix of excitement and heritage. Party at the famous beaches of North Goa, explore the Portuguese architecture in South Goa, and enjoy a romantic Mandovi River Cruise.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Goa

Pick up from Dabolim/Mopa Airport or Madgaon/Thivim Station. Transfer to hotel in North Goa. Evening free to stroll on Calangute Beach.

Day 02
North Goa Sightseeing

Full day tour of North Goa beaches: Calangute, Baga, Anjuna, and Vagator. Visit Fort Aguada and its lighthouse. Evening enjoy local nightlife.

Day 03
South Goa Sightseeing

Visit Old Goa Churches (Basilica of Bom Jesus, Se Cathedral). Visit Mangeshi Temple. Drive to Panjim for shopping. Evening Mandovi River Cruise with cultural show.

Day 04
Departure

Check-out from hotel. Transfer to Airport/Station for your return flight or train.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Rentals:

Renting a scooty is the best way to explore Goa. Carry a valid driving license.

Important Guidelines
  • Taxi: Goa taxi unions are strong; meters are rarely used. Fix prices before boarding or use our car.
  • Drugs: Drugs are illegal and strictly policed. Do not indulge.
