Overview

Escape to the "Queen of Satpura." This 4-day Pachmarhi package is designed for nature lovers and explorers. Discover the majestic Bee Falls, the ancient Pandav Caves, and the breathtaking sunset at Dhoopgarh. Whether you are trekking through the ravines or seeking blessings at the Jata Shankar Caves, Pachmarhi offers a refreshing break from city life amidst verdant forests.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Pachmarhi

Pick up from Pipariya Railway Station. Drive to Pachmarhi. Check-in to hotel. Evening visit to Catholic Church and Sunset Point (optional). Overnight stay.

Day 02
Bee Falls & Jungle Safari

Morning visit to Bee Falls (Jamuna Prapat), Rajat Prapat (Big Fall), and Handi Khoh. Post lunch, enjoy a jeep safari in Satpura National Park (optional). Overnight stay.

Day 03
Dhoopgarh & Caves Tour

Early morning visit to Dhoopgarh for sunrise (highest point in MP). Later explore Pandav Caves, Jata Shankar Caves, Gupt Mahadev, and Fairy Pool. Overnight stay.

Day 04
Departure

Morning breakfast and transfer to Pipariya Railway Station for return journey.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: Pachmarhi is the only hill station in central India and offers a unique mix of trekking, history, and wildlife. Our itinerary includes the best sunrise/sunset points and gives you ample time to relax, unlike other rushed itineraries.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Carry light woolens for winter (Nov-Feb). Comfortable walking shoes with a good grip are essential for trekking to waterfalls.

Permits:

Indian nationals generally do not need permits, but entry fees apply for certain viewpoints. We handle all local entry taxes.

Connectivity:

Network coverage is decent (Airtel/Vodafone works better) but can be patchy in the jungle areas. Enjoy the digital detox!

Nature:

Pachmarhi is eco-sensitive. Do not pluck flowers or litter in the forest or near waterfalls.

Important Jungle Guidelines
  • Safari Booking: Satpura National Park safaris are strictly regulated. We request you to confirm the trip 2 weeks in advance to secure safari slots.
  • Road Conditions: The roads within Pachmarhi are hilly and narrow. Our experienced drivers ensure a safe journey.
  • Check-in: Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM.
  • Waterfalls: The rocks near Bee Falls and Apsara Vihar can be slippery. Wear appropriate footwear and exercise caution.
