Overview

Rejuvenate your senses in the "Land of Strawberries." This 3-day escape takes you to the lush green hills of Mahabaleshwar and the panoramic plateaus of Panchgani. Enjoy boating in Venna Lake, savor fresh strawberries at Mapro Garden, and witness the sunset from Arthur's Seat. A perfect quick getaway for families and couples seeking cool weather and scenic views.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival & Venna Lake

Pick up from Pune/Mumbai Airport or Railway Station. Drive to Mahabaleshwar. Check-in to hotel. Evening visit to Venna Lake for boating and explore the local market for strawberries and chikki. Overnight stay.

Day 02
Mahabaleshwar Local Sightseeing

Morning visit to Arthur's Seat, Queen's Point, and Echo Point. Head to Mapro Garden for strawberry crush and lunch. Visit ancient Mahabaleshwar Temple and Panchganga Ghat. Evening leisure.

Day 03
Panchgani & Departure

After breakfast, drive to Panchgani. Visit the breathtaking Table Land (second largest mountain plateau in Asia), Sydney Point, and Parsi Point. Depart for Pune/Mumbai with sweet memories.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: Mahabaleshwar is one of the few hill stations that offers a mix of scenic beauty, great food, and accessibility. This package is designed to cover all major "Points" without rushing, giving you ample time to soak in the mist and enjoy the local flavors.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Carry light woolens or jackets for evenings, especially in winter. Comfortable walking shoes are a must for viewpoints.

Food:

Must-try: Strawberry with cream, corn pattas (bhutta), and Mapro products.

Shopping:

Buy fresh jams, preserves, honey, and leather chikki from the main market.

Travel:

The ghat section can be foggy at night. We plan travel to ensure daylight crossing for safety.

Important Hill Station Guidelines
  • Weather: Sudden rain and fog are common. Carry an umbrella or raincoat.
  • Traffic: Weekends and holidays can be crowded. Book in advance for confirmed hotel rooms.
  • Monkeys: Avoid eating in open cars or near temple areas as monkeys can be aggressive.
  • Eco-Friendly: Please do not litter plastic on the viewpoints or in the lake.
