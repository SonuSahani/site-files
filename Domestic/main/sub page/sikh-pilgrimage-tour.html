Spiritual Journey

Sikh Pilgrimage Tour

4 Days / 3 Nights Amritsar, Punjab 4.9 (Pilgrimage)

Overview

Embark on a divine journey to the spiritual heart of Sikhism. Visit Sri Harmandir Sahib (The Golden Temple), the holiest of Gurudwaras. Witness the patriotic fervor at the Wagah Border ceremony and pay homage to the martyrs at Jallianwala Bagh. This package offers a comfortable and guided experience of Amritsar's rich history and culture.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival & Wagah Border

Arrive at Amritsar Airport/Railway Station. Meet our representative and transfer to hotel. Afternoon drive to Wagah Border (Indo-Pak border) to witness the "Beating the Retreat" ceremony. Return to hotel. Overnight stay.

Day 02
Golden Temple & City Tour

Morning visit to Sri Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple). Visit Jallianwala Bagh to honor the martyrs. Afternoon visit to Durgiana Temple and Partition Museum. Evening free for shopping in local markets (Phulkari work, Juttis). Overnight stay.

Day 03
Tarn Taran Sahib / Goindwal Sahib

Day excursion to historical Gurudwaras around Amritsar: Sri Tarn Taran Sahib and Goindwal Sahib (known for the Baoli Sahib). Return to Amritsar in the evening. Overnight stay.

Day 04
Departure

Breakfast at hotel. Transfer to Airport/Railway Station for your return journey with spiritual memories.

The Spirit of Seva

"At the Golden Temple, it's not just the gold that shines—it's the spirit of humanity. Whether it's the 24/7 Langar (community kitchen) feeding thousands or the peaceful Gurbani echoing across the Sarovar, Amritsar touches your soul. The Wagah Border show is a stark contrast—loud, energetic, and brimming with patriotism. This tour is a journey of both inner peace and national pride."

Our View

Why we recommend this package: Amritsar is one of the most organized and welcoming cities for pilgrims. This itinerary covers all major sites without being rushed. We ensure your stay is close to the Golden Temple so you can visit early morning or late night to avoid crowds.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Cover your head (scarf provided) and wear modest clothes. Shoulders and knees must be covered in Gurudwaras.

Photography:

Allowed in the complex but restricted inside the main Sanctum. Respect the rules.
