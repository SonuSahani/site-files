Overview

The "Queen of Hills" meets the "Valley of Gods". This handcrafted itinerary takes you through the pine-scented trails of Shimla and the snow-capped peaks of Manali. From colonial architecture and mall road strolls to adrenaline-pumping river rafting and snow fights at Rohtang Pass, this trip has it all.

Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Shimla

Pick up from Delhi/Chandigarh Airport/Railway Station. Drive to Shimla (350 km from Delhi). Enjoy the scenic drive through timber trails. Check-in to Shimla hotel. Evening free to stroll on Mall Road and Ridge. Overnight stay.

Day 02
Kufri Excursion & Sightseeing

Morning visit to Kufri. Enjoy horse riding and yak riding (optional). Visit Himalayan Nature Park. Afternoon visit to Jakhu Temple, Christ Church, and Scandal Point. Overnight stay in Shimla.

Day 03
Shimla to Manali

Morning drive to Manali (260 km). En route visit Pandoh Dam, Hanogi Mata Temple, and Kullu Shawl Factory. Enjoy White Water Rafting in Kullu (optional). Arrive Manali, check-in to hotel. Overnight stay.

Day 04
Solang Valley / Rohtang Pass

Excursion to Solang Valley for adventure activities like Paragliding, Zorbing, and Skiing. Visit Rohtang Pass (subject to permit availability and weather). Return to Manali. Overnight stay.

Day 05
Manali Local Sightseeing

Morning visit to Hadimba Devi Temple, Vashisht Hot Springs, Van Vihar, and Tibetan Monastery. Evening free for shopping at majestic Mall Road. Overnight stay.

Day 06
Departure

Morning breakfast. Check-out and drive back to Chandigarh/Delhi for your onward journey. Tour ends with sweet memories.

Our View

Why we recommend this package: This is our most popular package because it offers the perfect balance. You get colonial history in Shimla and adventure in Manali. We use only comfortable vehicles because we know the mountain roads can be tiring.

Traveler's Guide & Tips

Clothing:

Heavy woolens are needed in winter (Dec-Feb). Light woolens in summer nights. Good walking shoes are essential.

Health:

Carry motion sickness pills. Drink plenty of water to avoid dehydration at high altitudes.

Important Guidelines
  • Rohtang Permit: Access to Rohtang Pass is strictly regulated by NGT. Permits are limited and issued usually 1-2 days in advance online.
  • Snow Point: If Rohtang is closed, vehicles go up to Gulaba or Solang Valley snow point.
