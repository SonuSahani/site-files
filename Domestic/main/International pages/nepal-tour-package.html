Frequently Asked Questions

The best time to visit Nepal is from September to November (autumn) and March to May (spring). These months offer clear skies and moderate temperatures, perfect for trekking and sightseeing.

No, Indian citizens do not need a visa to enter Nepal. However, you must carry a valid Passport or a Voter ID card for identification. Aadhar card is not always accepted at airports.

The official currency is the Nepalese Rupee (NPR). Indian Rupee (INR) is widely accepted, but denominations of ₹500 and ₹2000 are often restricted. It's best to carry ₹100 notes or exchange for NPR.

Yes, Nepal is generally very safe for solo travelers. The locals are exceptionally friendly and helpful. However, always inform your tour operator or hotel about your plans, especially when trekking.
